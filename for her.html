A letter

Mau,I don't always find the right words when you're in front of me. But I've been thinking a lot, and I need you to know this: I see you. I see everything you've been doing, quietly, without asking for anything in return.

These last few months have been the hardest of my life. And through all of it..you stayed. You didn't have to. You chose to. You held me together on the days I couldn't do it myself, and I was so lost in my own chaos that I forgot to turn around and ask how you were doing.

That was wrong of me. Not because I don't care, but because I do, more than I've been showing. When you'd try to tell me something and it turned into a fight, it wasn't because your feelings didn't matter. They always did. I was just too deep in my own head to hear properly.

You are not "too sensitive." Your little things are not little to me, not really. I'm sorry for ever making you feel that way. You deserve someone who listens first. I'm working on being that.

With everything I have, always — your person.
